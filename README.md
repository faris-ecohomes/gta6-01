**Palma Bay: Heat Wave** — a top-down open-world crime game in vanilla JavaScript and canvas, no build step and no network. Wheelman Dario Venn owes the Sorrento family $2,000, and fixer Marta Quill has three jobs that cover it: steal a gold Solana Coupe from the marina, run a package to the family warehouse past armed guards, and empty the Palma Bay Bank vault, then lose the heat and settle the debt. After the story, the city stays open with endless cab fares.

The city is a seeded 6×6-block island (~3,500 px) with named landmarks — Palma Customs (pay'n'spray), the hospital, the bank, the docks, a marina and pier, Coral Park, PBPD headquarters. It has lane-following traffic, wandering pedestrians who flee danger and phone in the crimes they witness, a six-star wanted system with up to six police interceptors that chase and ram, a bust meter, mouse-aim hitscan combat on foot and from the car, burning and exploding wrecks, a full HUD with minimap, dialogue, save/continue, and WebAudio-synthesised engine, siren and gunfire sounds. All names, art and story are original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
# (the hand-in program: mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts)
```

Controls: WASD move/drive · Shift sprint · E interact / enter cars · mouse aim, click to fire · Space brake · Esc pause · M mute.
